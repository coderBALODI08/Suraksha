Your Privacy Matters

🔒

Why is location requested?
Location is requested solely to route emergency responders to the correct scene and display relevant alerts on your community safety map.

When is location shared?
Your exact GPS coordinates are shared with emergency responders only when you explicitly press "Submit Report" or trigger "SOS Emergency".

Public Map Privacy Protection:
To protect your residential privacy, coordinates displayed on the public citizen map are gently obfuscated by 100–150 meters. Only authorized emergency officers receive exact coordinates.

Evidence Security:
Uploaded photos are securely stored with random UUID filenames. No private metadata (EXIF personal identifiers) is published to the public.

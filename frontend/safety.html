Community Safety & Emergency Preparedness

Crucial step-by-step guidance during critical emergencies. Stay calm, ensure personal safety first, and notify response personnel.

🔥

Fire Emergency Response

Dial 101 or 112
  • Evacuate immediately: Do not use elevators; use marked fire escape stairs.
  • Stay low under smoke: Toxic gases rise; crawl where air is cleanest.
  • Test doors before opening: Feel doorknobs with the back of your hand. If hot, do not open.
  • Report exact floor & room: Submit a report on SURAKSHA with landmark and people trapped.
🚗

Road Accident & Crash Response

Dial 108 (Ambulance) / 100 (Police)
  • Secure the scene: Turn on vehicle hazard lights; place warning triangles if safe.
  • Good Samaritan Protection: Indian law protects bystanders assisting accident victims from police harassment.
  • Do not move injured spinal victims: Unless there is imminent fire or explosion risk.
  • Pin location on SURAKSHA: Enable GPS to guide emergency ambulance navigation.
🏥

Sudden Medical Emergencies

Dial 108 Immediately
  • Assess responsiveness: Gently shake shoulders and ask loudly, "Are you okay?"
  • Check breathing & pulse: If unresponsive with no normal breathing, begin chest compressions (100–120 bpm).
  • Heat exhaustion: Move to cool shade, loosen clothing, elevate feet, offer cool water if conscious.
  • Clear the path: Have someone wait on the main road to flag down incoming paramedics.
⚠️

Suspicious Activity & Public Safety

Dial 112 / 1090 (Women)
  • Never confront suspects alone: Maintain safe distance and observe from well-lit areas.
  • Unattended items: Do NOT touch, kick, or open unattended bags or suspicious containers.
  • Note key identifiers: Note clothing color, height, vehicle number plates, and direction of travel.
  • Use SOS: If feeling directly threatened or followed, activate SURAKSHA SOS for immediate beacon broadcast.
🔎

Missing Person Guidance

Dial 100 or 1098 (Childline)
  • Zero waiting period: There is NO 24-hour waiting rule for missing children or vulnerable adults in India.
  • Recent photo: Have the most recent, clear photograph ready to attach on SURAKSHA.
  • Specific clothing markers: Color of shirt, shoes, birthmarks, and languages spoken.
  • Last known location: Report the exact spot, gate number, or transit station where last seen.
🎒

Emergency Kit Preparedness

Household Preparedness
  • Power bank & cables: Keep a charged 10,000mAh+ power bank ready at all times.
  • First Aid pouch: Bandages, antiseptic liquid, sterile gauze, burn ointment, ORS packets.
  • Emergency Contacts: Memorize 2 family contact numbers in case phone battery depletes.
  • Whistle & torch: Simple, non-electronic signaling tools during night hazards or power outages.
